Good to Know
How the journal is built

Twenty-six weeks, about six months. Each week has five daily pages, one weekly reflection and one coloring page.

Nothing is dated, so you can begin on any day and pick it back up after a break without feeling behind.

Printing tips

The pages are US Letter (8.5 × 11 in). On an A4 printer, choose “fit to page” or “scale to fit”.

If you color with markers, print the coloring pages single-sided so nothing shows through.

Using it on a tablet

Open the tablet version in GoodNotes, Notability or your favorite PDF app. Tap “Index” in the top corner of any page to return to the contents, or use the bookmarks in the sidebar to jump straight to a week.

A gentle note

You never need to complete every page. One word counts. So does a color, or a page you simply turned. Come back whenever it feels right.
