How to Use Your Workbook

There's no right way to work through these pages.

Start at the beginning or open to whatever catches your attention. Write a lot or write one sentence. Skip a question. Come back later. Change your answer.

You can print the full workbook or only the pages you want to use.

If you prefer to use it digitally, import the PDF into your preferred PDF annotation or note-taking app and write directly on the pages.

For printing
  • Choose the file size that matches your paper (A4 or US Letter).
  • Print at 100% / Actual Size for best results.
  • Standard printer paper works perfectly fine, or use slightly heavier paper if you prefer.
  • Consider test-printing one page before printing the full workbook.

And most importantly: there's nothing to get right here.
